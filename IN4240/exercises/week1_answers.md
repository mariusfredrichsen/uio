## Question 11
A test team consistently finds between 90 and 95% of the defects
present in the system under test. While the test manager
understands that this is a good defect-detection percentage, senior
management and executives remain disappointed, saying that the
test team misses too many bugs. Given that the users are generally
happy with the system and that the failures which have occurred
have generally been low impact, which of the testing principles is
most likely to help the test manager explain why some defects are
likely to be missed?

a. **Exhaustive testing is impossible**
b. Defect clustering
c. Pesticide paradox
d. Absence-of-errors fallacy

Answer: It is impossible to test everything since there are just too many things to be tested.
