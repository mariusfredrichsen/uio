# Distribution Shifts
## Distribution shifts
- train data distribution is not the same as test data distribution
- different distributions in the test and data can cause the models to fail
- types of distribution shift
    - covariate shift
    - label/target shift
    - output noise shift/concept shift
    - class-conditional/concept shift
    - full distribution shift
